## Owned implementation inventory

- `public_ast::is`: token/node, keyword, statement, declaration, expression,
  type-node, and literal predicates over canonical `SyntaxKind`; adjacent-family
  negative cases are pinned by `families_reject_adjacent_non_members`.
- `public_ast::factory`: generic canonical `Node<T>` construction plus
  identity-preserving no-change updates; `caller_identity_and_payload_kind_are_authoritative`
  and `update_reuses_unchanged_node_and_allocates_real_change` pin caller identity,
  payload-derived kind, and pointer reuse.
- `public_ast::utils`: zero-copy UTF-16 range and node text, half-open
  containment, and narrowest-node lookup; Unicode slicing and boundary behavior
  are pinned by the module tests.
- `public_ast::scanner`: direct re-exports of the canonical `scan`,
  `ScannedSource`, and `Scanner`; the module test pins token order, EOF, and
  source-backed lexeme borrowing.
- `public_ast::visitor`: `Visitor`, `visit_source_file`, and `visit_node` walk
  canonical child edges without parent links or a parallel tree; the module
  test pins parent-before-child and left-before-right source order.
- `public_ast::clone`: `clone_node` and `clone_node_with_id` deep-copy the
  canonical owned payload while preserving ranges and either preserving or
  caller-assigning the root identity; tests pin structural equality,
  allocation distinction, and caller identity.
